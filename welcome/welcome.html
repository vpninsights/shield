Welcome to Shield

Protection is already on. Here is what you now have, and the two minutes of setup worth doing.

Set up

1
Pin the icon

Click the puzzle piece in your toolbar and pin Shield. The number on the icon is how many trackers it blocked on the page you are on.

2
Pick a level

Standard blocks ads and trackers, cleans links and declines cookie banners. Strict adds more: it blocks scripts from other sites, hides your browser fingerprint, and clears a site's cookies when you close it. Some sites may need a click on "Site broken?" in Strict.

3
Breached-password warning

Warns you when a password you type has appeared in a known data breach. Your password stays on the page. Only 5 characters of its hash are sent to Have I Been Pwned. Off until you turn it on.

What is in the popup

Privacy grade

A to F for the page you are on, and the companies that tried to track you, grouped (Google, Meta, Criteo and so on).

Cookie banners

Shield presses "reject" or "necessary only" for you. It never presses accept.

Trackers tab

Everything it blocked, with an Allow button, plus other sites running scripts that you can block.

VPN tab

Shows your address and whether it matches your VPN. Shield is not a VPN. It checks the one you already use.

Weekly report

Open it from the popup footer, or wait for the Monday notification. Numbers only, kept on your device. You can turn the notification off in Settings.

If a site breaks

Open the popup and press "Site broken?". It turns protection down step by step, and you can copy a report to send us.

Open all settings Run the free privacy check

Your privacy first. Shield needs permission to read and change pages so it can block things. It has no account and no analytics, and it never records or sends where you browse. It is not a VPN and does not hide your IP address.
