Shield

Settings stay on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on the VPN companion.

Protection level

Standard blocks ads and trackers and cleans links. Strict also blocks scripts from other sites and cleans cookies when you close a site. Changing any switch below makes it Custom.

Block ads, trackers and session recordersOur list of well known advertising, analytics and tracking services.
Remove tracking from linksStrips utm_*, fbclid, gclid and similar before the page loads.
Block scripts from other sites by defaultLike NoScript. You can allow a site in the popup.
Open the secure version of sitesOff by default. Browsers have their own HTTPS-first mode, and sites without HTTPS will not load.

Privacy signals

Open the real linkSkips the tracking redirect that Google, Facebook, YouTube and others put on links, and opens AMP pages as the real page.
Warn about breached passwordsOff by default. When you finish typing a password, checks it against known breaches. Your password stays on this page: only the first 5 characters of its hash are sent to Have I Been Pwned.
Fingerprint shieldHides canvas, audio and hardware readings that sites use to recognise your browser. On in Strict. A few sites that draw or edit images may look different.
Monday privacy reportA notification each Monday with last week's numbers. Calculated on your device.
Decline cookie bannersPresses "reject all" or "necessary only" on the common consent pop-ups. It never presses accept.
Global Privacy ControlTells sites not to sell or share your data. Many are legally required to honor it.
Hide WebRTC addressesStops sites reading your local network address.
Drop the Referer sent to other sitesOff by default. Some sites need it to show their own images.

Cleanup

Clear cookies when you close a siteWhen the last tab of a site closes. Sites on your keep list are skipped.
Clear site storage as wellLocal storage, IndexedDB and caches. Chrome, Edge and Brave only.
Keep me logged in onOne site per line, for example mail.example.com

VPN companion

Warns you if your IP address changes, which can mean your VPN dropped. When on, the extension asks vpninsights.com for your address every 5 minutes. That request reveals your IP to us like any website visit. We do not log it.

Watch my IP addressOff by default.
My VPN addressConnect your VPN, then press the button. You are warned if your address stops matching it.

Custom rules

Block your own list of domains, or import a public list. Domains only: one per line, a hosts file (0.0.0.0 ads.example.com) or the simple Adblock form (||ads.example.com^). Start a line with @@|| to allow a domain. Rules that need paths or page elements are skipped.

My rules
Import a listFrom a file on your computer, or paste its address. Imported lists are kept as they are and are not updated by themselves. The list is data only: no code is loaded.

About

Shield by VPNInsights

Made by VPNInsights. Author: Iam Waqas. MIT licence.

No account, no analytics, no ads. Everything runs on your device. The only network request we make is the optional VPN companion check.

Why we ask for permissions: read and change pages (to block and clean them), cookies and browsing data (cleanup), web requests (to count what was blocked), privacy (WebRTC), notifications (VPN warnings).

It is not a VPN. It does not hide your IP address. Pair it with a VPN we have tested.
